## Overview

Computational Intelligence (ELEC 8330) is an advanced graduate course in the Master of Engineering (MEng) program at the University of Windsor. This course explores the intersection of biology-inspired algorithms and computational problem-solving, focusing on neural architectures, fuzzy systems, and genetic optimization.

### Course Objectives

The curriculum encompasses several key intelligent computing domains:
- **Neural Networks**: Understanding Hebbian learning, Associative Memories (AM), and Bidirectional Associative Memories (BAM).
- **Self-Organizing Maps**: Implementing SOM and Learning Vector Quantization (LVQ) for unsupervised and competitive learning.
- **Fuzzy Logic Systems**: Developing fuzzy sets, reasoning engines, and Mamdani/Sugeno inference systems.
- **Evolutionary Computing**: Leveraging Genetic Algorithms (GA) for global optimization and search space exploration.
- **Deep Learning Foundations**: Exploring Radial Basis Function (RBF) networks and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).

### Repository Purpose

This repository represents a curated collection of study materials, reference books, course assessments, and technical MATLAB implementations compiled during my academic journey. The primary motivation for this archive is **to preserve knowledge for continuous learning and professional reference**.

**Why this repository exists:**

- **Knowledge Preservation**: To maintain organized access to comprehensive study materials beyond the classroom.
- **Continuous Learning**: To support lifelong learning by enabling easy revisitation of fundamental CI principles.
- **Academic Documentation**: To authentically document my learning journey through Computational Intelligence.
- **Community Contribution**: To share these resources with students and researchers who may benefit from them.

> [!NOTE]
> All materials were created, compiled, and organized by me during the Winter 2024 semester as part of my MEng degree requirements.
